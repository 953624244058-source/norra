About NORRA

Quiet clothes for loud lives

NORRA was founded on a simple idea: clothing should be considered, not consumed. Every piece is designed to outlast the season it was made for — honest materials, restrained silhouettes, and a palette that never goes out of style. This project reimagines NORRA as a full digital-first fashion brand, from product to campaign.

We work with small ateliers, use natural fibres wherever possible, and design every collection around versatility rather than novelty. Our customers keep NORRA pieces for years, not seasons.

How Hybrid Encryption Works

  1. ECC (X25519) establishes a shared secret.
  2. BLAKE2b derives a symmetric encryption key.
  3. ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts and authenticates the message.
  4. Tampering with the ciphertext should cause verification to fail.
